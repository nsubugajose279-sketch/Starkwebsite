Uganda's First BMW M8 Competition

A High-Performance Machine

The BMW M8 Competition is a high-performance luxury vehicle offered as a coupe, convertible, or Gran Coupe (a four-door sedan). It is powered by a 617-horsepower twin-turbo 4.4-liter V8 engine, capable of accelerating from 0 to 60 mph in about 3 seconds.

Engine: A 4.4-liter, twin-turbo V8 producing 617 horsepower and 553 lb-ft of torque.

Transmission: An 8-speed M Sport automatic transmission with M Drivelogic for adjustable shift aggression.

Drivetrain: A rear-biased all-wheel-drive (M xDrive) system that can send up to 100% of power to the rear wheels.

Brakes: Available with high-performance carbon ceramic brakes for superior stopping power.

Performance & Competition

BMW M8 Competition Hits 60 in 2.5 Seconds - The BMW M8 Competition is a fast, powerful luxury grand tourer, not a dedicated time attack car. While it can achieve impressive 0-60 mph times of around 2.5 to 3.2 seconds, its heavy size and weight make it difficult to achieve peak lap times on a track without significant driver effort.

This BMW M8 Competition participated in the time attack wars that took place in Jinja at Banana Beach where it beat many Japanese cars (JDMCARS). Although it was ultimately won by a Mitsubishi Lancer Evo10 due to a late launch by the owner @k9Rick on TikTok, the performance was impressive.

About Us

Stark Car Meets is Uganda's fastest-growing automotive community, dedicated to bringing together sports car enthusiasts, drifters, and rally fans. Based in Uganda , we organize regular events that showcase the best of Uganda's automotive culture.

Our mission is to create a safe, inclusive environment where car lovers can connect, share knowledge, and experience the thrill of high-performance vehicles. Whether you're a seasoned driver or just passionate about sports cars, there's a place for you in our community.

From organized track days and drifting competitions to casual meetups and rally events, Stark Car Meets offers diverse experiences for all automotive enthusiasts.
